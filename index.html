Enter your cube's MAC address

GAN cubes encrypt their data with a key based on the cube's Bluetooth MAC address, and this browser can't read it automatically. You only need to do this once.

How do I find it?
  • Easiest: turn on chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features (in Edge: edge://flags), restart the browser, and reconnect. The address is then read automatically.
  • Or open chrome://bluetooth-internals/#devices, click Start scan, turn a face to wake the cube, and copy the address listed next to its name.
  • The last four characters of the cube's name (GANi4_AEC3) are the end of the address.
